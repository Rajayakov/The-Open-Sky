Dear friends,

In 2022 we walked in the Himalaya with a group of soul seekers from all around the world, and we felt it clearly: the mountains themselves are a sacred medicine. We are going back to receive a message, guidance and the blessings of the Himalayas. We warmly invite you to come with us on this journey, one that can touch you deeply in heart, mind and soul.

In spring 2027 we will walk the Annapurna Circuit together, from Pokhara over the Thorong La pass to Muktinath, a holy place that pilgrims have walked to for centuries. The route winds through the sacred peaks of Annapurna, the Goddess of Abundance. It is a fourteen-day program with nine days of walking. We go as a small group with a mountain guide and porters, and there is time every day for prayer, singing, sharing and deep inner listening.

Spring is a beautiful time to walk here. The hillsides glow with rhododendrons, the national flower of Nepal, and the snow peaks stand sharp and bright against a deep blue sky. The days are warm in the sun, the nights are cold and quiet, and the high ridges still wear their winter snow.

Muktinath is sacred to both Hindus and Buddhists, and its name is often translated as ‘Lord of Liberation’. Water from 108 springs pours from the rock, a small flame burns beside the water, and all five elements are said to meet in this one place.

A pilgrimage is simply a walk with a purpose.

Some people come with a question. Some come to put something heavy down. Some just love the mountains and want to share them with kind company. All of that is welcome, and you do not need to follow any particular tradition to join us.

Itinerary

  1. Day1
    PokharaMar 28 · ~820 m

    Arrive in Pokhara and check in at the hotel in your own time. Overnight at the hotel.

  2. Day2
    PokharaMar 29 · ~820 m

    Group introductions, an opening talk and a safety briefing. Gear shopping and renting, permits and paperwork. Sharing circle. Overnight at the hotel.

  3. Day3
    Pokhara to ChameMar 30 · ~2,670 m · 8–9 h

    Our journey begins. A day of driving on mountain roads. In the evening we soak in the hot springs of Chame, then hold our opening circle with intention setting, meditation and prayer.

  4. Day4
    Chame to Upper PisangMar 31 · ~3,300 m · 5–6 h

    Walking through forest, with views of Annapurna II.

  5. Day5
    Upper Pisang to ManangApr 1 · 3,519 m · 5–6 h

    The upper trail, with wide views of the whole Annapurna range.

  6. Day6
    Manang, rest dayApr 2 · 3,519 m · 3–4 h

    A hike above the village to help the body adjust, then back to Manang for the night.

  7. Day7
    Manang to KhangsarApr 3 · 3,734 m · 4–5 h

    Walking toward the Tilicho valley.

  8. Day8
    Khangsar to Tilicho Base CampApr 4 · ~4,140 m · 5–6 h

    Crossing scree slopes.

  9. Day9
    Tilicho Lake to Shri KharkaApr 5 · ~4,080 m · peak 4,920 m · 6–8 h

    A morning climb to one of the highest lakes in the world. Overnight in Shri Kharka (~4,080 m).

  10. Day10
    Shri Kharka to Yak KharkaApr 6 · ~4,110 m · 4–5 h

    Little altitude gain. Walking toward the pass.

  11. Day11
    Yak Kharka to Thorong PhediApr 7 · ~4,600 m · 3–4 h

    Our last camp before the pass. Rest and preparation.

  12. Day12
    Thorong La to MuktinathApr 8 · ~3,800 m · peak 5,416 m · 8–9 h

    The crossing of the pass, the highest and fullest day of the journey. We sleep in Muktinath (~3,800 m), and the high-altitude banya (Russian sauna) is waiting.

  13. Day13
    Muktinath, day at the templeApr 9 · ~3,800 m

    The Muktinath temple, sacred to Hindus and Buddhists, the 108 springs, and the Gumba Samba and Jwala Mai temples. We rest, share, and close our circle with prayer.

  14. Day14
    Muktinath to PokharaApr 10 · ~820 m · 6–8 h or ~20 min

    Back by jeep via Jomsom, or by a short flight. Those joining the deepening program stay in Muktinath.

What each day looks like

Morning qigong

Gentle movement and breathing to wake up the body for the day of walking.

Walking

At your own rhythm, sometimes talking, sometimes in silence.

Ritual, prayer and meditation

At special places along the trail we pause for short moments of ritual, prayer and meditation, to arrive in the place and listen inside.

Intention setting

At the start, each person sets an intention. It can be a question, a wish or a prayer. We return to it on the pass and again in Muktinath.

Songs and chanting

Kirtan and mantra, song circles, heart songs and medicine songs. We both sing, on our own and together, and you are invited to sing with us.

Heart sharing circle

Every evening we gather to listen to each other and share from the heart.

Six days in Muktinath

After crossing the pass, the body has done its hard work and the mind is often open and quiet. For those who want to go deeper, we stay on in Muktinath. The days are slower: meditation and silence, energy and body practices, intention work, and two plant medicine ceremonies guided by Yakov, with time in between to rest and share.

Where it happens
A comfortable hotel in Muktinath with very good food, hot showers, a ceremony hall and a high-altitude banya (Russian sauna).

Safety and organization

The route is led by a professional mountain guide with long experience on high-altitude Himalayan treks. Everything is planned in advance, and our team takes care of the details. Once you sign up, we will send you the full list of what to bring and all the information you need to make this journey safe.

Guide and team

A professional mountain guide. ACAP and TIMS permits are arranged in advance by the organizers.

Light all the way

Porters carry your main bags for the entire trek. You carry only what you need for the day.

Medical

A first-aid kit with altitude medication, including for altitude sickness, stays with the guide.

Built-in acclimatization

A rest day in Manang and a gradual gain in height, with a helicopter emergency plan along the route.

Important for participants
  • Medical insurance covering helicopter evacuation up to 6,000 m (paid separately, about $100)
  • Normal or good physical condition and readiness for multi-day trekking
  • A short video call with us before you join
