# Materials — The Open Sky

The permanent material library. Every future page draws its physical surfaces from here — stone, paper, wood, bronze, fabric, earth. Nothing generic, nothing glossy, nothing futuristic.

Assets are **not downloaded yet**. This file is the curated shortlist an Art Director would hand to production — verified sources, real license terms, honest gaps flagged where a source didn't have the exact match.

## How to read each entry

`Preview` links to the live asset page (view it there — nothing is downloaded or embedded here). `Blend mode` + `Opacity` are starting points consistent with how `mountains.astro` already layers grain (`mix-blend-mode: overlay`, ~2.5%) — tune per section, never let a material read as a graphic.

## Sources — honesty check

- **Poly Haven** (polyhaven.com) — verified via API. CC0. Scanned at 8K minimum, downloadable 1K–8K. Primary source for Stone, Wood, Fabric, Earth.
- **ambientCG** (ambientcg.com) — verified. CC0 / Public Domain. Primary source for Paper and Bronze & Metal (Poly Haven's own "metal" category is industrial corrugated iron, not luxury metal — wrong register for this project).
- **Textures.com** — real site, but its free tier is effectively gone: new accounts no longer receive free download credits, and most content requires paid Premium Credits. Not used as a source below; flagged so no one wastes an evening registering for it.
- **Textures.one** — could not verify this domain resolves or exists; it doesn't appear in any current index of free-texture sites. Not used. **CGBookcase.com** substituted where a third stone/wood source was useful (real, free, permissive license).
- **Unsplash** — used only where noted, for photographic mood references (not tileable PBR maps) per Unsplash's free commercial license (no attribution required for standard use).

## Paper — 5 materials

Poly Haven has no paper category at all — every entry below is ambientCG (generic paper scans, un-named by subtype) or a curated Unsplash photographic reference. **Grading is required** to hit "handmade" / "Japanese" / "aged" specificity — flagged per entry rather than pretending an exact scan exists.

**Usage notes — read before use:** ambientCG's Paper00X series are generic fiber scans, not literally "handmade" or "washi" — warm-tint and desaturate toward the site's bone/parchment palette before use. The two Unsplash entries are photo collections, not seamless tiles — pick a single flat, evenly-lit shot and treat it as a background photograph (e.g. a card's base layer), not a repeating pattern.

## Bronze & Metal — 5 materials

All five below are ambientCG. **Caveat:** only Metal008 and Metal018 were confirmed by description ("bronze, bumpy copper, scratches" / "bronze, dark erosion, rusted") — the other three surfaced from a broader brass/patina category search and are unverified by content; ambientCG's numeric names don't self-describe.
